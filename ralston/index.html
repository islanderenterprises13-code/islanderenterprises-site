Who qualifies

  • Gross household income of at least 3x the monthly rent ($6,897/mo)
  • Roommates may combine income
  • Co-signers are welcome
  • Every adult who will live in the home applies and is on the lease
  • Screening includes credit, background and rental history, applied the same way to every applicant

We will never ask for a deposit before an in-person showing. If someone does, it isn't us.

How to apply

  1. Message us through our Zillow listing for 1050 Ralston St, or email admin@islanderenterprises.net.
  2. Tour the home in person at a time that works for you.
  3. Apply on Zillow. Every adult submits an application, which includes the screening.
  4. Sign and move in. Once approved, sign the lease, pay the deposit and first month, and pick up your keys.

Questions

Is the home furnished?

Your choice. Furnished includes a couch, TV, rug, a queen bed and mattress in each bedroom, and a dining table. Unfurnished includes the appliances and washer/dryer.

Is there a dishwasher?

No. The kitchen has a new refrigerator, oven and built-in microwave.

Where do I park?

Street parking in front of the home.

Who handles yard work?

The landlord maintains the grounds. The front yard is dry-scaped and low-maintenance.

Can I rent for less than 12 months?

We prefer 12-month leases. Ask us about shorter terms, which are priced higher.

Are pets allowed?

No pets. Assistance animals are not pets and are handled under fair housing law.
